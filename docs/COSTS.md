# Cost model — initial planning

No paid resources have been purchased or configured. The real operating cost cannot be stated as zero without selecting the user's Google plan, AppSheet licensing, messaging volume and provider setup.

## Cost categories to verify before launch
- Google account or Workspace plan, storage and administration.
- AppSheet licenses for platform administrators, company administrators and drivers.
- WhatsApp Business messaging charges and any provider/relay fees.
- Optional domain and monitoring services.
- Backup storage and operational support.
- A webhook verification relay if Apps Script cannot securely verify the provider's signature.
- Professional privacy/legal advice where needed.

## Lowest-cost evaluation path
1. Keep live WhatsApp disabled.
2. Use GitHub Actions and synthetic data for code tests.
3. Test Apps Script with a non-production workbook in an account the owner is authorized to use.
4. Confirm AppSheet licensing with current official pricing before inviting real users.
5. Confirm Meta's current pricing and template requirements for the actual market and message categories.
6. Estimate monthly cost from expected active users, deliveries and message volume before agreeing to a pilot.

Do not purchase a plan or incur charges without owner approval. All prices must be checked against official provider pages at the time of decision; the repository does not yet contain a verified, dated price quote.
