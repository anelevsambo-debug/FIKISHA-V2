# Privacy and compliance checklist

This is a technical checklist, not legal advice or a claim of compliance. A qualified adviser should verify the requirements applicable to the actual business, data flows, retention periods and operating countries before launch.

## Data handled by the intended product
Delivery names, phone numbers, pickup/drop-off addresses, instructions, driver assignments, status events, consent records and optional proof-of-delivery evidence can identify individuals and reveal location or activity.

## Minimum safeguards
- Collect only information necessary to coordinate the delivery.
- Explain why customer contact information and proof-of-delivery evidence are collected.
- Record channel-specific opt-in where required; honor opt-out requests.
- Restrict access to company workbooks and Drive evidence.
- Never make proof-of-delivery folders publicly accessible.
- Define retention and deletion rules for completed deliveries, messages and evidence.
- Avoid storing provider tokens or secrets in Sheets.
- Provide a process for correcting inaccurate contact or delivery information.
- Define how to handle access requests, deletion requests, complaints and security incidents.
- Document service providers and cross-border data flows.
- Restrict developer/central script owner access and audit privileged access.
- Train staff not to export or share customer lists unnecessarily.

## Legal review topics
Verify current Botswana and Eswatini data-protection and privacy requirements, electronic communications and marketing rules, consumer obligations, business records and retention, liability for lost or damaged goods, and any rules affecting international transfers of personal data. Confirm current regulator guidance rather than relying on this checklist alone.

## Launch gate
Do not claim the service is compliant until the business-specific legal review, notices, consent flows, retention policy, access controls and incident procedure have been completed and evidenced.
